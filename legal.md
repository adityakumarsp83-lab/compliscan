# The Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011

**Ministry of Consumer Affairs, Food and Public Distribution**  
*(Department of Consumer Affairs)*  
**NOTIFICATION**  
New Delhi, the 7th March, 2011  

**G.S.R. 202 (E).**—In exercise of the powers conferred by sub-section (1) read with clauses (j) and (q) of sub-section (2) of section 52 of the Legal Metrology Act 2009 (1 of 2010), the Central Government hereby makes the following rules, namely:

---

## Chapter I: Preliminary

### 1. Short Title and Commencement
1. These rules may be called the **Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011**.
2. They shall come into force on the **1st day of April, 2011**.

### 2. Definitions
In these rules, unless the context otherwise requires:
- **(a) "Act"** means the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 (1 of 2010).
- **(b) "dealer"** in relation to any commodity in packaged form, means a person who, or a firm which, carries on directly or otherwise, the business of buying, selling, supplying or distributing any such commodity whether for cash or for deferred payment or for commission, remuneration or other valuable consideration, and includes a commission agent who carries on such business on behalf of any principal, but does not include a manufacturer who manufactures any commodity which is sold or distributed in a packaged form except where such commodity is sold by such manufacturer to any other person other than a dealer.
- **(c) "lot"** means:
  - (i) in the case of packages which have been stored, the total number of such packages stored; and
  - (ii) in the case of packages which are on or at the end of the packing line, the maximum hourly output of packages.
- **(d) "manufacturer"** in relation to any commodity in packaged form, means a person who, or a firm which, produces, makes or manufactures such commodity and includes a person or firm which puts, or causes to be put, any mark on any packaged commodity, not produced, made or manufactured by him or it, and the mark claims the commodity in the package to be a commodity produced, made or manufactured by such person or firm, as the case may be.
- **(e) "Maximum permissible error"**, in relation to the quantity contained in an individual package, means an error in deficiency which, subject to the provision of these rules, does not exceed the limits specified in the First Schedule.
- **(f) "net quantity"**, in relation to commodity contained in a package, means the quantity by weight, measure or number of such commodity contained in that package, excluding the packaging or wrapper.
- **(g) "packer"** means a person who, or a firm which, pre-packs any commodity, whether in any bottle, tin, wrapper or otherwise, in units suitable for sale whether wholesale or retail.
- **(h) "principal display panel"**, in relation to a package, means the total surface area of the package where the information required under these rules is to be given in the following manner, namely:
  - (i) all the information could be grouped together and given at one place; or
  - (ii) the pre-printed information could be grouped together and given in one place and online information grouped together in another place.
- **(i) "quantity"** in relation to commodity contained in a package, means the quantity by weight, measure or number of such commodity contained in that package.
- **(j) "retail dealer"** in relation to any commodity in packaged form means a dealer who directly sells such packages to the consumer and includes, in relation to such packages as are sold directly to the consumer, a wholesale dealer who makes such direct sale.
- **(k) "retail package"** means the packages which are intended for retail sale to the ultimate consumer for the purpose of consumption of the commodity contained therein and includes imported packages:
  - *Provided that* for the purposes of this clause, the expression 'ultimate consumer' shall not include industrial or institutional consumers.
- **(l) "retail sale"**, in relation to a commodity, means the sale, distribution or delivery of such commodity through retail sales shops, agencies or other instrumentalities for consumption by an individual or a group of individuals or any other consumer.
- **(m) "retail sale price"** means the maximum price at which the commodity in packaged form may be sold to the consumer and the price shall be printed on the package in the manner given below:
  - `'Maximum or Max. retail price Rs/ .......inclusive of all taxes'` or `'MRP Rs/ .........incl., of all taxes'` after taking into account fractions:
    - Less than 50 paise to be rounded off to the preceding rupee.
    - Above 50 paise and up to 95 paise to be rounded off to 50 paise.
- **(n) "section"** means a section of the Act.
- **(o) "Schedule"** means a Schedule appended to these rules.
- **(p) "standard package"** means a package containing the specified quantity of a commodity.
- **(q) "wholesale dealer"** in relation to any commodity in packaged form means a dealer who does not directly sell such commodity to any consumer but distributes or sells such commodity through one or more intermediaries.
- **(r) "wholesale package"** means a package containing:
  - (i) a number of retail packages, where such first-mentioned package is intended for sale, distribution or delivery to an intermediary and is not intended for sale direct to a single consumer; or
  - (ii) a commodity sold to an intermediary in bulk to enable such intermediary to sell, distribute or deliver such commodity to the consumer in smaller quantities; or
  - (iii) packages containing 10 or more retail packages provided that the retail packages are labeled as required under the rules.
- **(s) Words and expressions** used herein and not defined but defined in the Act, shall have the meanings respectively assigned to them in the Act.

## Chapter II: Provisions Applicable to Packages Intended for Retail Sale

### 3. Applicability of Chapter II
The provisions of this Chapter shall not apply to:
- **(a)** Packages of commodities containing quantity of more than 25 kg or 25 litres (excluding cement and fertilizer sold in bags up to 50 kg); and
- **(b)** Packaged commodities meant for industrial consumers or institutional consumers.

**Explanation:**
- **Institutional Consumer:** Transport operators, Airways, Railways, Hotels, Hospitals, or any other service institutions buying packaged commodities directly from the manufacturer for institutional use.
- **Industrial Consumer:** Consumers buying packaged commodities directly from the manufacturer for industrial use.

### 4. Regulation for Pre-packing and Sale of Commodities
No person shall pre-pack or cause or permit to be pre-packed any commodity for sale, distribution, or delivery unless the package bears thereon or has securely affixed thereto a label with mandatory declarations required under these rules.
- *Explanation:* Unpriced packages inside manufacturer premises are not a violation, provided all packages leaving the premises bear the retail sale price declaration.

### 5. Standard Pack Sizes (Second Schedule)
Commodities specified in the **Second Schedule** must be packed in prescribed standard quantities.
- *Note:* The initial proviso permitting non-standard pack declaration stood withdrawn w.e.f. 01.07.2012 (vide GSR 748(E)).

### 6. Declarations to be Made on Every Package
1. Every package must bear a plain, definite, and conspicuous declaration as to:
   - **(a) Name and complete address:**
     - Manufacturer (or manufacturer and packer if distinct).
     - Importer name and address for imported packages.
     - *Explanation I:* Name/address without qualifying words is presumed to be the manufacturer.
     - *Explanation II:* Brand owner appearing as marketer shall be held liable as deemed manufacturer.
     - *Explanation III:* For food articles, the provisions of Prevention of Food Adulteration Act (PFA) / FSSAI rules apply instead of this sub-rule.
   - **(b) Commodity identity:** Common or generic name; for multi-product packages, the name and quantity of each item.
   - **(c) Net quantity:** By standard unit of weight, measure, or count.
   - **(d) Date of packing:** Month and year of manufacture, pre-packing, or import.
     - *Exceptions:* Food articles (PFA rules apply), seeds (Seeds Act, 1966 applies), cosmetics (Drugs and Cosmetics Rules, 1945 apply).
     - *Exempted commodities:* Bidis, agarbatti/incense sticks, and domestic LPG cylinders (14.2 kg or 5 kg PSU bottled).
   - **(e) Retail Sale Price (MRP):** Inclusive of all taxes. (State Excise laws apply for alcoholic beverages).
   - **(f) Dimensions:** Dimensions of finished pieces where size is relevant.
   - **(g) Other required particulars.**
2. **Consumer Care Contact Details:** Name, address, phone number, and email (if available) of the grievance office or person.
3. **Stickers:** Individual stickers for altering/making declarations are prohibited.
   - *Exception:* A sticker showing a **revised lower MRP** may be affixed without covering the original MRP.
4. **Multi-piece units:** Declarations must appear on the main wrapper/outer pack and indicate contents, or appear on individual units.

### 7. Principal Display Panel (PDP) – Area, Letter & Numeral Sizes
- For packages $\le$ 5 cm³, information may be given on a firmly affixed card or tape.
- Letter height $\ge$ 1 mm (or $\ge$ 2 mm if blown/molded/embossed). Letter width $\ge$ 1/3 of height (except for '1', 'i', 'I', 'l').

#### Minimum Height of Numerals (Table-I & Table-II)

| Net Quantity (Weight / Volume) | PDP Area (Length / Area / Count) | Normal Case (mm) | Blown / Formed / Molded / Embossed (mm) |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Up to 200 g / ml | Up to 100 cm² | 1.0 mm | 2.0 mm |
| Above 200 g/ml up to 500 g/ml | Above 100 cm² up to 500 cm² | 2.0 mm | 4.0 mm |
| Above 500 g/ml | Above 500 cm² up to 2500 cm² | 4.0 mm | 6.0 mm |
| — | Above 2500 cm² | 6.0 mm | 6.0 mm |

### 8. Declaration Placement & Clear Area
- Must appear on the Principal Display Panel.
- Surrounding net quantity: clear space of at least 1× numeral height (top/bottom) and 2× numeral height (left/right).
- Returnable beverage bottles: MRP may appear on the crown cap or bottle.

### 9. Manner of Declarations
- Must be legible, prominent, and in contrasting color (not mandatory if blown/molded).
- Must not require reading through liquid contents.
- Language: Hindi (Devnagri script) or English (additional languages permitted).

### 10. Declaration of Name and Address
- Complete postal address of factory or registered office with PIN code.
- Actual corporate or registered business name.

### 11. General Quantity Provisions
- Exclude tare/wrapper weight.
- Cannot qualify quantity with expressions like *"when packed"*, except for commodities listed in the **Third Schedule**.

### 12. Units & Expression of Quantity
- Solid/viscous $	o$ Mass (weight).
- Liquid $	o$ Volume.
- Linear/area $	o$ Length / Area.
- Number $	o$ Count (`N` or `U`).
- Prohibited misleading terms: *"minimum"*, *"not less than"*, *"average"*, *"about"*, *"approximately"*.

### 13. Standard Units (SI Units Only)
- Weight: gram (g) for $< 1	ext{ kg}$; kilogram (kg) for $\ge 1	ext{ kg}$.
- Volume: millilitre (ml) for $< 1	ext{ L}$; litre (L) for $\ge 1	ext{ L}$.
- Length: centimetre (cm) for $< 1	ext{ m}$; metre (m) for $\ge 1	ext{ m}$.
- Prohibited terms: dozen, score, gross, great gross.

### 14–17. Special Dimensional Rules
- **Rule 14:** Bed-sheets, fabrics, dhotis, sarees, towels $	o$ Finished dimensions and count.
- **Rule 15:** Where dimensions/weight dictate price $	o$ Both weight and dimensions.
- **Rule 16:** Foils, facial tissues, toilet paper $	o$ Usable sheet count and dimensions.
- **Rule 17:** Container items (bags, boxes, cups) $	o$ Count and dimensions/holding capacity.

### 18. Duties of Wholesale and Retail Dealers
- Cannot sell at a price exceeding MRP.
- Altering, smudging, or obliterating MRP is strictly prohibited.
- If taxes are revised, price increases cannot exceed the exact tax differential and must follow mandatory statutory notifications.
- Retailers under VAT/TOT dealing in weight/volume pre-packs must maintain an **Accuracy Class III electronic weighing scale** (division $\le 1	ext{ g}$) with printed receipt facility for consumer self-verification.

### 19–23. Inspection, Enforcement & Deceptive Packages
- **Inspection (Rule 19):** Legal Metrology Officers draw samples per the **Fifth Schedule** and test per the **Sixth Schedule**.
- **Lot Approval:** Statistical average of samples $\ge$ declared quantity, and zero samples exceed Maximum Permissible Error (MPE).
- **Deceptive Packages (Rule 23):** Packages designed with slack fill or misleading extra space must be repacked/relabeled or seized, unless justifiable for machine packing or transit protection.

## Chapter III: Provisions Applicable to Wholesale Packages

### 24. Declarations on Wholesale Packages
Every wholesale package must conspicuously declare:
1. Name and complete address of the manufacturer / packer / importer.
2. Identity of the commodity.
3. Total number of retail packages contained OR total net quantity (weight, volume, or count).

---

## Chapter IV: Export and Import

### 25. Restrictions on Sale of Export Packages in India
Export packages cannot be sold in India unless repacked or relabeled in full compliance with Chapter II retail provisions. Non-compliant packages are liable to seizure.

---

## Chapter V: Exemptions

### 26. Exempted Packages
The rules do not apply to:
- **(a)** Net quantity of $10	ext{ g}$ or $10	ext{ ml}$ or less (sold by weight/measure).
- **(b)** Fast food items packed by restaurants or hotels.
- **(c)** Drug formulations governed under the Drugs (Price Control) Order, 1995.
- **(d)** Agricultural farm produce in packages exceeding $50	ext{ kg}$.

---

## Chapter VI: Registration of Manufacturers, Packers & Importers

### 27. Mandatory Registration
- Every manufacturer, packer, or importer must apply to the Director or Controller within **90 days** of commencement of business.
- **Fees:**
  - Registration fee: ₹500
  - Alteration in certificate: ₹100
- Authority must return incomplete applications within 7 working days or issue the registration certificate.

### 28–30. Shorter Address & Public Register
- Shorter address may be approved by Director/Controller if sufficient for consumer identification (Rule 28).
- Register is maintained and open for public inspection free of charge (Rule 29).
- Director circulates state-wise list to State Controllers for enforcement sampling (Rule 30).

---

## Chapter VII: General & Penalties

### 31. Advertisements
Any advertisement mentioning retail sale price must declare net quantity in the same font size as the price.

### 32. Penalties
- **Contravention of Rules 27 to 31:** Fine of ₹4,000.
- **Contravention of any other rule (residual):** Fine of ₹2,000.

### 33–34. Relaxation & Repeal
- Central Government retains power to relax rules for reasonable periods.
- Repealed the *Standards of Weights and Measures (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 1977* with standard saving clauses.

### The Sixth Schedule: Net Quantity Determination Procedures
1. **Equipment Standards:** Officers use verified working standards. Manufacturer's equipment may be used only if its MPE is $\le 20\%$ of product MPE.
2. **Tare Weight Evaluation:**
   - Single empty tare measured: If tare $\le 0.3 	imes 	ext{MPE}$, tare is applied across all samples.
   - If tare $> 0.3 	imes 	ext{MPE}$, 5 tare containers measured. If $(	ext{Max} - 	ext{Min}) \le 0.4 	imes 	ext{MPE}$, average tare is used.
   - If difference $> 0.4 	imes 	ext{MPE}$, destructive or 100% opening procedure applies per lot size.
3. **Liquids, Length & Count:**
   - Liquids: Via specific gravity calculation or direct decanting into calibrated measures.
   - Length: Measured using calibrated steel tape.
   - Count: Physical manual counting across sample packages.

### The Seventh Schedule: Reporting Formats
